# Question

What does residual sugar measure, and is it the same thing as the quality score?

# Modified Answer

Wine notes: Residual sugar is a laboratory measurement of the sugar left after fermentation, which is recorded in grams per litre (source: measurements, page 4). It is not the same thing as the quality score (source: measurements, page 4). The quality score is a sensory score provided by tasters and is not computed from residual sugar or any other laboratory column (source: dataset, page 2).
